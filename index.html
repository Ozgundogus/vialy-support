Vialy Support

Vialy is a tracker for GLP-1 medicines and peptides: log doses and vials, get reminders, and follow your weight and progress. This page answers common questions and tells you how to reach us.

Email us: support@crascodes.com
In the app: Profile → Report a problem adds your device ID, so we can find your account faster. We usually reply within two business days.

Do I need an account?

Yes. At the end of setup you sign in with Apple or Google, so your data is kept safely and stays with you when you change phones. Your setup answers are saved and linked to your account when you sign in.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Vialy on your iPhone, or Profile → Manage plan in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid but Pro isn't on

Open Profile → Restore purchases with the same Apple ID you bought with. If that doesn't help, email us with the date of purchase.

My reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are on in Profile → Notifications and in iOS Settings, and that Focus or Sleep mode isn't silencing Vialy. Dose reminders are sent at the time you set for each protocol.

Does Vialy read my Apple Health data?

Only if you allow it, and only to show your trends. Apple Health data stays on your iPhone; it is never uploaded or shared.

Can Vialy tell me what dose to take?

No. Vialy is a tracking tool, not a medical device. It never suggests or calculates a dose. Log the dose your prescriber gave you.

How do I delete my data?

Use Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything stored with it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
